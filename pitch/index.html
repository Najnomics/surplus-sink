Surplus Sink

Private orderflow leftover shouldn't vanish. It should sink into LPs.

Public swaps pay a tax. Private fills mint a SurplusReceipt. Relayers creditSurplus — donate() into the same pool.

LIVE ON UNICHAIN SEPOLIA  ·  uhi10-surplus-sink.vercel.app

Opening: refund address is the pool. Live ssVOL/ssUSD.

The problem

RFQ leftover goes to the solver

  • Private orderflow (RFQ, solver, TEE) often improves on quote.
  • That surplus sits in the relayer / solver wallet — not the pool.
  • Public toxic flow still hits LPs with no extra bill.
  • Attestation-only fees don't create a receipt the pool can collect against.

The gap

Fair Flow prices the block. Sold Backrun prices the residual hunt.

Neither makes the pool the refund address for private surplus.

Distinct from Fair Path tax and Sold Backrun auction.

The insight

Treat the pool as the refund address. Mint a receipt on private fills. creditSurplus donates it home.

Public flow is billed. Private flow is receipted. Either way, LPs are the sink — not the solver's leftover wallet.

Same pool Public tax SurplusReceipt EIP-712 / TEE donate()
Two lanes, one sink.

How it works · the hook

Tax public. Receipt private. Sink both.

01 · afterSwap

Split the lanes

Public: skim a tax and donate(). Private: mint a SurplusReceipt keyed to the fill.

→
02 · relayer

Prove surplus

TEE attestation or EIP-712 signed quote vs fill. Relayer calls creditSurplus.

→
03 · settle

Donate home

Hook takes the credited amount and donate() to in-range LPs. Receipt consumed.

Bad receipts revert. Expired receipts cannot be credited. The pool never trusts a naked number.

creditSurplus is the money shot in the demo.

The trust model

The hook does not invent surplus. It settles a receipt.

01 · private mark

Who is private

Relayer / TEE flag in hookData. Public is the default — taxed, not receipted.

→
02 · quote

EIP-712 or TEE

Signed quote vs executed amount. Surplus = improvement the solver would have kept.

→
03 · consume

One receipt, once

BadReceipt / Expired revert. No double credit into donate.

Judges: we don't guess leftover — we settle signed surplus.
